# ctdev is tracked in its own Linear team

ctdev's work lives in the Linear team **CTDev** (`CTD`), a sub-team of Conner Technology, rather
than in a project on the shared `CON` team. The template's version of this decision gives each repo
one project and a status update at the end of every session. ctdev's work already spanned several
projects, and on the shared team it was only findable by a `ctdev` label that every issue had to
carry. A team makes the repo's work visible on its own board and in its own identifiers, and the
parent team still sees it.

## Consequences

The 45 ctdev issues moved from `CON` on 2026-09-24; their old identifiers and URLs still resolve.
The `ctdev` label was deleted, since the team does its job. There are no end-of-session status
updates; the issues are updated as work happens. `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` holds the details.
